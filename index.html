The Painters Desk

Hue runs around the wheel; paints nearer the rim are lighter, nearer the center darker. Tap a paint to see its complement and neighbors for underpainting, shading and glazing. Dashed outline = wash, glowing outline = fluorescent.

Choose paints ·

Tick a whole range, or open it and tick individual paints. Selections from different ranges and brands show on the wheel together. While searching, a range checkbox selects only the matches.
