Welcome to The Painters Desk

A free colour wheel and paint toolkit for miniature painters. See 5,467 paints from 18 brands, from Army Painter and Citadel to Golden artist acrylics, laid out by hue and lightness, match them across brands, plan ramps and colour schemes, and share how you painted it.

11 November 2026

  • BetterSee your ramp on a model part. Every Ramps technique except Drybrushing and Underpainting now has buttons under its preview to show the ramp on something you might paint: NMM on a sword, axe, shield, armour, helmet or goblet; skin on a face, hand or torso; zenithal on a cloaked figure, rock or cube; edge highlights on an armour plate, pauldron or tank panel; glazes on a cloak or flat panel; gems as round, teardrop or square cut; and OSL as a glow, a lantern or eye lenses. Each technique remembers your choice.
  • FixedA proper "page not found" page. Old or mistyped links used to show a blank white page. They now show a page in your theme with links back to the colour wheel, paints and guides.
  • FixedCorrect paint count. The site now says 5,467 paints. The old figure of 5,475 also counted the 8 Pro Acryl basing textures, which aren't paints.
  • BetterTighter security. The site now tells browsers to always use a secure connection and blocks other sites from showing it inside a frame.
  • BetterNew link preview and app icon. Links to the site shared on Discord, Facebook and elsewhere now show a Neon 1984 card with the current paint, brand and theme counts. The installed app icon and the default favicon are now the Neon 1984 sun. Paint Pot keeps its watercolour icon.
  • BetterWelcome page brought up to date. It now mentions the shopping list, Plan for a project, the paint quick look, the shorter match lists and all 16 themes.

Tap any technique ramp or colour square for the details and paints that would fill its gaps.
  • NewShopping list. Tap ☆ Add to shopping list on a suggested paint or on any paint you select on the wheel. Your list is on My paints, saved to your account: copy it as text for the shop, show it on the wheel, and tap Got it when you buy one to move it into your collection.

Palettes and recipes have a Plan it with my paints button too.
  • BetterQuick look at suggested paints. Tap a suggested paint's name or swatch to see why it's suggested, the closest paints you already own and its matches in other ranges, with buttons to add it, show it on the wheel or open its paint page.
  • NewThree new themes. Steampunk Workshop (brass pipes, gauges and gears), Egyptian Tomb (pyramids at dusk, lapis and gold) and Void Station (deep space above a blue world). Pick one from the Theme button.
  • BetterEvery other theme, refreshed. New backdrops that sit behind the top of the page: misty pines for Wildwood, gold-rimmed jade peaks for Dwarven, light shafts and kelp for Eldritch Abyss, a celestial chart for Arcane Library, aurora over ice for Frostforge, lava and black rock for Infernal, and a huge sun over dunes for Desert Wastes. Hobby Bench and Sci-fi Lab get tidier buttons and panels. Desert Wastes text is easier to read too.

Grimdark has a new backdrop of hive spires in ember-lit smog, a riveted iron band and cast-brass buttons.   • BetterShorter, closer matches in other ranges. With 18 brands on the site, the match list for a paint had grown to about 60 chips, some of them far off. It now shows the best match from each range: conversion-chart ✓ matches that are within ΔE 25 of the swatch, otherwise the nearest paint of the same type (so no washes for a base paint) within ΔE 12, with no paint listed twice. The metallic section is folded away, and a Show all button brings back the full list. The paint equivalent pages (Paints → any paint) use the same shorter list, with the full one folded underneath.
  • BetterTruer swatches from real painted cards. 78 swatches have been checked against photos of hand-painted swatch cards and corrected where they were clearly off: 39 AK 3rd Gen (many blues, teals and greens were far too dark), 13 Pro Acryl, 13 Two Thin Coats, 8 AMMO IONIC, 4 Kimera and Vallejo Ice Yellow. Swatches are still estimates.

They change colour with the light and with your primer, so the swatch (taken over white primer from Scale75's own photos) is only a rough guide. They count as metallics on the wheel.

  • PaintsMore Golden Heavy Body, with better swatches. 33 more Heavy Body colours: 8 fluorescents, 14 iridescent metallics, 6 interference colours and 5 new colours such as Azo Gold and Naples Yellow Deep. The existing Heavy Body swatches have been re-sampled from Golden's own swatch photos, so many look truer now. Swatches are still estimates.

Colour scheme

Tap a paint on the wheel, then pick how its partner colours are chosen. The wheel marks a hue band for each colour in the scheme and suggests a paint for each one.

How far from each target hue a paint can sit and still count. Wider finds more paints, narrower keeps the scheme strict.

Paint types on the wheel

Washes, inks and effect paints only show their colour over a basecoat, so they can crowd out better complements. Untick a type to hide it from the wheel and the neutrals.

Palette from a photo

Pick a photo you like (artwork, a scene, a painted miniature) and get the paints closest to its main colours. A photo can't show a metallic finish, so metallic and pearl paints are left out. Your photo stays on this device: it is only uploaded if you add it to a saved palette card.
