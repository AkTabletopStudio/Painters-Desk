What's new

The main changes to The Painters Desk, newest first. Found a bug or have an idea? Use the Feedback button on any page.

26 October 2026

  • BetterSmoother centre blend. The blend in the middle of the wheel now runs evenly from dark to light on each side, so bright and muted paints of the same shade mix together instead of making light and dark stripes.
  • FixedNeon 1984 signs spell real words. The neon signs in the Neon 1984 background now read painting words like Zenithal, Highlight, Glaze, Wash, NMM and OSL instead of jumbled letters.
  • BetterTidier guides page. The Guides page now starts with colour theory, lists the painting techniques from easiest to hardest with a preview of each ramp and a line on what it covers, and groups the conversion charts into from Citadel and to Citadel, with shorter titles throughout.
  • NewTechnique guides. Every Ramps technique now has a full step-by-step guide: non-metallic metal, OSL, gems, skin, and new guides to zenithal highlights, edge highlighting and glazing. Each one explains the technique step by step, the colour theory behind the ramp, how the Ramps mode builds it and the common mistakes, with the closest paints in every brand. All seven are linked from Ramps mode, and each guide has a button that opens the wheel on that technique.
  • BetterGuides are easier to find. A new Guides button sits in the top bar. In Ramps mode, NMM, OSL, skin and gems link to their guide, and a selected paint links to its own paint page and a conversion chart for its brand.
  • NewColour theory guide. A new colour theory guide explains hue, value and saturation, the colour wheel and the classic colour schemes (complementary, analogous, split complementary and triadic) with example paints, and how The Painters Desk wheel places paints, picks complements and neighbours, and matches paints between brands. Find it under Guides, or from the link under the wheel.

25 October 2026

  • NewCommission painters. Painters who take commissions can now list themselves from their account page, with photos of their work, styles, prices, turnaround and links to their shop or socials. Find them under Recipes & tutorials in Commission painters, and message a painter through the site: they get your message by email and reply to you directly, and your email is only shared with the painter you write to.
  • FixedPaint pages and guides follow your theme. The paint pages and guides now use the look you picked on the colour wheel (Neon 1984, Grimdark, Forest, Dwarven or Paint Pot), with the same backgrounds, fonts and icons.
  • NewPaint guides. New conversion charts (Citadel to Vallejo, Army Painter, Pro Acryl, AK and Scale75, and back again) list every paint with its chart-listed equivalent and its nearest colour side by side, and new guides pick the best paints in each brand for non-metallic metal, skin, OSL and gems. Each brand page now opens with a short guide to the brand and how its colours compare to Citadel.
  • FixedArmy Painter washes. The Warpaints Fanatic washes (the "Tone" paints) now count as washes, so they're hidden with the other washes in Paint types and no longer turn up in ramps.
  • NewA page for every paint. Each of the site's paints now has its own page at paintersdesk.com/paints, with its closest matches in every other brand, so you can search the web for something like "Vallejo equivalent of Mephiston Red" and land straight on the answer, or share a link to a paint. Each page also lists the public tutorials, recipes and palettes that use the paint, and See it on the colour wheel jumps to that paint on the wheel. Swatch colours and matches are still estimates.

24 October 2026

  • NewReport button and photo checks. Every public recipe, tutorial, palette and profile now has a Report link, so you can tell us about anything that breaks the new site rules. Uploaded photos are checked automatically before they're saved, and you need a confirmed email to upload. Moderators can hide or remove photos and block accounts.
  • NewPalette from a photo. Tap From a photo on the colour wheel and pick any picture: the site pulls out its main colours and finds the closest paint for each, from every brand or only your own paints. Tap the photo to add a colour from a spot you like, swap in a different paint if you prefer, then show them all on the wheel or save them as a Palette card in Recipes & tutorials (signed in). Your photo never leaves your device unless you choose to add it to the card.

23 October 2026

  • BetterOne Metallics section per brand. In the paint lists, metallics no longer hide inside mixed ranges such as Citadel Base and Layer, Warpaints Fanatic, Speedpaint or Model Air. Each brand now has one Metallics section, split by the range each paint comes from, and Tamiya's pearl and iridescent sprays have their own Pearls section. Your collection, saved wheel and recipes are unchanged.

22 October 2026

  • BetterTwo Thin Coats grouped by triad. The Two Thin Coats ranges now follow Duncan Rhodes' own chart: every triad paint sits under Triad shadows, midtones or highlights (the greys, bones, browns and skins included), plus Brights, Metallics, Washes and Glazes. Your collection and recipes keep these paints.
  • FixedMissed metallics. Grey Knights Steel and 16 other metallics whose names don't sound metallic (Army Painter coloured metallics such as Mithril, Fairy Dust and Glittering Loot, plus two Tamiya sprays) now count as metallics, so the Paint types button hides them with the rest.

  • FixedPrivacy policy link. The Privacy policy link at the bottom of the page showed a "site can't be reached" error once the site had been saved for offline use. It opens properly again.
  • NewRecipes and tutorials are now different things. A recipe is one colour: a short list of layers from base to highlight, shown as a colour strip. A tutorial is a whole model, step by step, with a level, time and model, and its steps can use recipes. Ramps, NMM and Blends now save as recipes, and the wheel shows recipes that use the paint you pick.
  • PaintsScale75. 190 paints: Scalecolor, Fantasy & Games, Instant Colors and the Scalecolor Artist range.
  • PaintsTamiya. 286 paints: XF flat and X gloss acrylics, plus the AS, PS and TS spray cans. Clears, thinner and flat base are left out.

The Painters Desk

Hue runs around the wheel; paints nearer the rim are lighter, nearer the center darker. Tap a paint to see its complement and neighbors for underpainting, shading and glazing. Dashed outline = wash, glowing outline = fluorescent. Switch to Ramps to build a shadow-to-highlight ramp from any paint for non-metallic metal, object source lighting, gems, skin, zenithal highlights, edge highlights or glazing blends. New to colour theory? Read how the colour wheel works.

Paint types on the wheel

Washes, inks and effect paints only show their colour over a basecoat, so they can crowd out better complements. Untick a type to hide it from the wheel and the neutrals.

Swatch colours are estimates and will differ from the real paints. Many Vallejo Game Color swatches and some Pro Acryl and AK 3rd Gen swatches are sampled from Antinea's photo databases of airbrushed bottle caps. Turbo Dork swatches are sampled from Turbo Dork's promotional colour image; colour-shift paints show only their main colour. Tamiya swatches are sampled from Tamiya's printed paint chip charts. Scale75 swatches are sampled from Scale75's colour charts, except Scalecolor Artist, which is sampled from a painter's hand-painted swatch sheet. Green Stuff World swatches are sampled from Green Stuff World's acrylic equivalence chart and 2025 Color Chart; colour-shift paints show only their main colour. Cross-brand matches are close, never exact; they draw on the Two Thin Coats Paint Comparison Chart 2024, the Army Painter Warpaints Fanatic conversion chart, the AK 3rd Generation Acrylics equivalence table (2021) and the Herrick Games hobby paint comparison chart, plus a nearest-colour calculation.
