What's new

The main changes to The Painters Desk, newest first. Found a bug or have an idea? Use the Feedback button on any page.

21 October 2026

  • BetterSmoother centre disc. When you select a paint, the centre disc now blends two ramps, the complements and your paint with its neighbours, each running dark to light and meeting where their shades match.
  • PaintsTone Pro fix. Removed the extra "Main" entry from each Citadel Tone Pro colour. The real range is five tones per colour (1 darkest to 5 lightest), and Main was a near-copy of tone 3.
  • NewUpdate emails. Choose to get an occasional email about new features, from the sign-up form or the Emails panel on your account page. It's off unless you opt in, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • NewPaint types. Choose which kinds of paint show on the wheel and in the neutrals: metallics, contrast paints, washes, inks, effects and technicals. This replaces the Hide metallics button.
  • BetterFaster wheel. The colour wheel is much quicker to draw and move around with big collections.
  • BetterNeon 1984 is the default look. New visitors choose a theme when they first arrive, and you can change it any time with the Theme button.
  • BetterTheme icons. Each theme has its own icon on the welcome page and in the nav.

19 October 2026

  • NewSite themes. Pick a look from the Theme button: Paint Pot, Neon 1984, Grimdark, Wildwood or Dwarven. Paint colours stay the same in every theme.
  • NewBlends mode. Build your own blend from complements and neighbours on the wheel. Pin the steps you like and the rest recalculate, then save it as a tutorial.
  • PaintsTurbo Dork. 80 paints: Metallic, TurboShift and ZeniShift. Colour-shift paints show their main colour only.
  • BetterSafer ticking on My paints. Ticking or unticking a whole range now asks first, and an Undo bar lets you take it back.
  • FixedPro Acryl S13 now uses Monument's official name, Dark Sea Ben.

18 October 2026

  • NewRamps mode. Build a shadow-to-highlight ramp from one paint for non-metallic metal, object source lighting, gems, skin, zenithal highlights, edge highlights or glazing.
  • NewSuggested paints. My paints suggests paints that fill the technique and colour gaps in your collection.
  • NewEmail and password accounts. Sign up with an email as well as Google, Discord or GitHub, with confirmation and password-reset emails.
  • NewPublic profiles. Show your favourite paints and your recipes and tutorials on your own profile page.
  • NewUpvotes, views and saves. Upvote recipes and tutorials with the paint bottle, see how many people viewed them, and keep the ones you like in your Saved tab.
  • NewWelcome page. A quick tour of each part of the site. Tap the Painters Desk logo to get back to it.
  • NewFeedback button on every page, for bugs and ideas.
  • BetterWheel centre gradient. Selecting a paint fills the centre of the wheel with a blend from its complements through to its neighbours.
  • BetterThe wheel remembers. It opens on the paints you last had selected, or on your collection if you're signed in.
  • BetterWater in tutorial steps, so you can show thinning ratios.
  • BetterTidier wheel. The neutrals panel folds away, and adding ticked paints to your collection asks first.
  • PaintsVallejo True Metallic Metal (80 paints) and the Army Painter John Blanche range (40 paints).
  • FixedPro Acryl Expert Acrylics group renamed from Expert Artist, and Phthalo spelling corrected.

17 October 2026

The Painters Desk

Hue runs around the wheel; paints nearer the rim are lighter, nearer the center darker. Tap a paint to see its complement and neighbors for underpainting, shading and glazing. Dashed outline = wash, glowing outline = fluorescent. Switch to Ramps to build a shadow-to-highlight ramp from any paint for non-metallic metal, object source lighting, gems, skin, zenithal highlights, edge highlights or glazing blends.

Choose paints ·

Tick a whole range, or open it and tick individual paints. Selections from different ranges and brands show on the wheel together. While searching, a range checkbox selects only the matches.

Paint types on the wheel

Washes, inks and effect paints only show their colour over a basecoat, so they can crowd out better complements. Untick a type to hide it from the wheel and the neutrals.

Many Vallejo Game Color swatches and some Pro Acryl and AK 3rd Gen swatches are sampled from Antinea's photo databases of airbrushed bottle caps. Cross-brand matches are close, never exact; they draw on the Two Thin Coats Paint Comparison Chart 2024, the Army Painter Warpaints Fanatic conversion chart, the AK 3rd Generation Acrylics equivalence table (2021) and the Herrick Games hobby paint comparison chart, plus a nearest-colour calculation.
