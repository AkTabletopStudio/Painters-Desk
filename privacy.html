Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

The Painters Desk (paintersdesk.com) is run by AK Tabletop Studio. This page explains what we store when you use the site, why, and how to remove it.

Using the colour wheel

You can use the colour wheel without an account. It runs in your browser and we don't collect anything about it. The site saves a copy of itself on your device so it works offline. There are no adverts, analytics or tracking cookies.

If you create an account

You sign in with Google, Discord or GitHub. Your first sign-in creates your account. From that service we receive and store:

  • your name (used as your display name),
  • your email address, if the service has verified it (used to join your sign-in methods into one account; we don't send you email),
  • your profile picture link,
  • an ID the service gives your account, so we recognise you next time.

We never see or store your password for those services.

What you add

  • Your paint collection: the paints you tick. Only you can see it.
  • Recipes and tutorials: text, paints, mix ratios, links and photos. Private ones are visible only to you. Public ones, with your display name and picture, can be seen by anyone.
  • Comments on public recipes. These are public, with your display name and picture.
  • Photos: shrunk in your browser before upload. Each photo has a long random address. Photos in a private tutorial aren't listed anywhere, but anyone you give a photo's address to can open it.

Cookies

Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. A short-lived cookie is also used during sign-in to protect it from forgery. Neither is used for tracking or advertising.

Where it's stored and who sees it

Everything is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site, in their database and file storage. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, and we use it only to run the site. The site's moderators can remove public recipes and comments that break the rules.

Removing your information

You can edit or delete your recipes, photos and comments at any time. To delete everything, open your account page and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, sign-in links, collection, recipes, photos and comments.

Children

Accounts are for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
